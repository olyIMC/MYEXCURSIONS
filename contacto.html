FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions we receive from travelers.

01 How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 2–4 weeks in advance for most excursions. For peak season or custom programs, 6–8 weeks is ideal. Last-minute requests are welcome — we'll do our best to accommodate.

02 Are your tours truly private?

Yes. Every program we arrange is exclusively for your party. No strangers, no shared vehicles, no group tours. Just you, your companions, and your personal guide or driver.

03 What's included in the price?

Prices typically include your private vehicle, driver or guide, reserved tickets where applicable, and all taxes. Meals, gratuities, and personal expenses are not included unless specified.

04 Can you accommodate large groups?

Absolutely. We work with couples, families, and groups of all sizes. For larger parties, we coordinate multiple vehicles and guides to ensure a seamless experience.

05 What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations made 14+ days before the service receive a full refund. Within 14 days, policies vary by service. We'll always provide clear terms before you confirm.

06 Do you offer services at cruise ports?

Yes — shore excursions are our specialty. We operate at hundreds of ports worldwide and guarantee your timely return to the ship.
