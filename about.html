Our Founders

Passionate Explorers

Meet the people behind the experiences — a dedicated team of travel experts who bring your dreams to life.

Tim Harwood

President & Owner

Following an early career in banking, Tim found his true passion and moved to the cruise industry in 1986. He joined NCL as a Shore Excursion Manager onboard the SS Norway.

His shipboard career spanned 9 years with Royal Viking Line, Sea Goddess, and Ocean Cruise Line. In 1995, he was asked to join Silversea Cruises to head up their Shore Excursion Department, and later recruited by Radisson (now Regent) Seven Seas Cruises to run their global Shore Excursion Department until the end of 2004.

Between 1986 and 2004, he travelled extensively, visiting over 100 countries. With this knowledge, he founded MyExcursions in early 2005.

Dinora Harwood

Vice President & Owner

Dinora brings to MyExcursions over 25 years of experience in finance, accounting, and logistics. She graduated in 1987-89 with two Bachelor degrees — one in Marketing and the other in International Business.

Initially concerned about the risks, she quickly saw that they had created a niche in the market, providing clients top-notch service when it matters most.

She wears several hats — running a family, a home, and enjoying her workouts — and she is the back office of MyExcursions. She confirms services and handles the financial aspects, ensuring each booking is treated with the highest level of quality.
